# Password Policy

**Organisation:** Adunni Fabrics & Fashion (fictional small business, used for this exercise)
**Version:** 1.0
**Author:** Ayishat Aiyepe Adunni

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets minimum requirements for passwords used on company accounts and devices, to reduce the risk of unauthorised access (see Risk #5, business email phishing/credential theft, in the Risk Assessment).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all company accounts: business email, business WhatsApp, the shared laptop, router admin panel, and any business software accounts (e.g. accounting tools).

## 3. Policy

### 3.1 Password requirements
- Minimum 12 characters.
- A mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols, or a passphrase of at least 4 unrelated words.
- No reuse of the same password across multiple accounts.
- No use of easily guessed values (business name, "password123", birthdays, phone numbers).

### 3.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- MFA/two-factor authentication must be enabled on business email and WhatsApp Business where the service supports it.
- Where possible, authenticator-app codes are preferred over SMS codes.

### 3.3 Storage and sharing
- Passwords must not be shared between staff members or written on visible notes.
- A password manager should be used to store and generate passwords, rather than reusing a memorable one.

### 3.4 Changing passwords
- Passwords must be changed immediately if a device is lost, an account is suspected to be compromised, or a staff member who knew the password leaves the business.
- Default passwords (e.g. on the Wi-Fi router) must be changed before the device is put into use.

### 3.5 Account lockout
- Where supported, accounts should lock or alert after repeated failed login attempts (see the SSH Attack-and-Defend Lab for a technical example of this control).

## 4. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually or after any security incident.

*This is a sample policy written for a fictional business as part of a GRC practice exercise, loosely aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A control 5.17 (Authentication information) and NIST SP 800-63B password guidance.*

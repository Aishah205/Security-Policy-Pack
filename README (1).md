# Security Policy Pack — Small Business

Two sample information security policies written for a fictional small business (Adunni Fabrics & Fashion), as a GRC practice exercise. Both are designed to be realistic for a low-budget small business rather than a large enterprise, and both tie back to specific risks identified in a companion [risk assessment](https://github.com/Aishah205/risk-assessment-small-business).

## Contents

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Acceptable Use Policy](./Acceptable_Use_Policy.md) | Sets rules for how staff use company devices, accounts, email, and Wi-Fi |
| [Password Policy](./Password_Policy.md) | Sets minimum password strength, MFA, and credential-handling requirements |

## Why these two

Both policies address risks flagged in the risk assessment: shared logins, no MFA, weak/reused passwords, and lack of staff awareness. Writing the policy is the governance step that follows identifying the risk — the register says what's wrong, the policy says what staff are expected to do about it.

## Framework alignment

Both documents reference the relevant ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls and, for the Password Policy, NIST SP 800-63B guidance, so they can be traced back to a recognised standard rather than being arbitrary rules.

## Limitations

These are sample policies written for a fictional business as a learning exercise, not policies implemented or audited at a real organisation.

# Acceptable Use Policy

**Organisation:** Adunni Fabrics & Fashion (fictional small business, used for this exercise)
**Version:** 1.0
**Author:** Ayishat Aiyepe Adunni

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out acceptable use of company devices, accounts, and networks (the laptop, business email, business WhatsApp, and Wi-Fi) to protect the business, its customers, and its staff from security and reputational harm.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all staff and any contractor given access to company devices or accounts.

## 3. Policy

### 3.1 Devices and logins
- Each staff member must use their own login on the shared laptop; logins must not be shared.
- Devices must be locked when unattended, even for short periods.
- Only approved software may be installed on company devices.

### 3.2 Accounts
- Business email and WhatsApp Business must use a unique password and two-factor authentication where available.
- Passwords must not be written down in visible locations or shared between staff.
- Staff must report a lost or stolen device, or a suspected compromised account, to the business owner immediately.

### 3.3 Internet and Wi-Fi
- The shop Wi-Fi admin password must not be shared with customers; a separate guest network is provided for customer use.
- Staff should avoid accessing personal, high-risk websites (e.g. unknown downloads) on company devices.

### 3.4 Data handling
- Customer order and payment information must only be used for business purposes.
- Business data must be backed up weekly, as set out in the Risk Assessment.
- Customer data must not be shared with third parties without the owner's approval.

### 3.5 Email and messaging
- Staff should be cautious of unexpected links or attachments, even from known contacts, and should verify unusual payment or order requests by phone before acting.
- Suspicious messages should be reported to the business owner, not deleted silently.

## 4. Consequences of non-compliance

Breach of this policy may result in a discussion with the business owner, additional training, or, for serious or repeated breaches, restricted access to company accounts and devices.

## 5. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually or after any security incident.

---

*This is a sample policy written for a fictional business as part of a GRC practice exercise, modelled on common small-business acceptable use policies and loosely aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A control 5.10 (Acceptable use of information and other associated assets).*
